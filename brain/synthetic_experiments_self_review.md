## Cross-check: Does the rewritten §4 support the central argument?

The paper's central argument is: *"A trustworthy RAG system for CPS incident intelligence cannot be built by stacking retrieval, generation, and provenance as independent modules. Instead, provenance metadata must enter the retrieval-and-ranking loop, generated claims must be verified against retrieved evidence before presentation, and the entire evidence-to-answer chain must be recorded in a tamper-evident manifest."*

The rewritten §4 supports this argument in three ways:
1. **Synergy evidence** (§4.2 middle): B4 outperforms B3 because provenance-aware retrieval improves the evidence quality fed to verification.
2. **Ablation evidence** (§4.4): Each component addresses a distinct failure mode — provenance for source integrity, verification for claim consistency, ledger for auditability. Removing any one component degrades a specific axis without degrading others.
3. **Cost evidence** (§4.5): The synergies do not come at prohibitive cost — bounded overhead under 200ms and 3KB.

This is a stronger argument structure than the original directional-arrows table, which lacked synergy and ablation evidence.
